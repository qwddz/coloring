Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Animosaic Colors is a coloring game with cute animals for children and their grown-ups, published by Dmytro Zinenko, Ukraine (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not ask for a name, e-mail, location, contacts or any other personal information. It does not read your photos or files.

Data that stays on your device

Your colored pictures (the picture, the colors you chose, the mode and whether it is finished), your drawings from the drawing mode, small preview images of them, and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are stored on the device. We never receive them and cannot see them. Android may include this app data, except drawings, in your own Android/Google backup, which is managed by the system and your Google account, not by us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Saving to the gallery

When you tap “Save to gallery”, the app creates a PNG image of your colored picture and writes it to your device’s gallery, in the folder Pictures/Animosaic Colors. This happens only when you ask for it, and the image stays on your device. On Android 10 and older, the system may ask for permission to write to storage for this; the app uses it only to save that image and never reads your photos or other files. On Android 11 and newer no permission is needed.

Sharing

“Share” opens the standard sharing sheet of your device, after a parental check, with the PNG image of your picture. Where the image goes next is decided by you and the app you pick. We do not receive the image, and we do not see who you share it with.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we never receive card or payment details. To confirm and restore purchases the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID created on the device, purchase information (product, date, store receipt), and basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and country/currency for prices. This data is not linked to your name or e-mail by us and is used only to unlock and restore the full version. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Downloading pictures

Some pictures are downloaded from our content server, hosted by a content delivery provider. As with any internet request, the provider processes the device’s IP address and request details to deliver the files and protect the service. We do not use this to identify you.

Children

The app is designed for children and their parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no ads, no social features and no chat. Purchases, sharing and links out of the app are behind a parental check (a simple question for an adult).

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking, and no crash-reporting service in this version.
  • No reading of your photos, files, contacts or location.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond the services named above, which act only to provide the app’s features.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. You can delete local data by uninstalling the app, and pictures saved to the gallery by deleting them there. To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, or about anything in this policy, contact us at contact@animosaic.app; we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

«Поделиться» открывает стандартное окно отправки вашего устройства, после родительской проверки, с PNG-изображением вашей картинки. Куда оно пойдёт дальше, решаете вы и приложение, которое вы выберете. Мы не получаем изображение и не видим, с кем вы им делитесь.
